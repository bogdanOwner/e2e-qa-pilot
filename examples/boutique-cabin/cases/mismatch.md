Title: Book Chalet 4 when the request times out
Preconditions: the guest opens the Cabana page at /?demo=timeout, from the Booking timeout demo link.
1. Set check-in to 4 Aug 2026 and check-out to 5 Aug 2026, then click Search.
   Expected: Chalet 4 is listed at 497 EUR, with 15% off and free cancellation.
2. Press Increase quantity on the Chalet 4 rate priced at 497 EUR, then click Reserve Now.
   Expected: the selection shows Chalet 4 and a total of 497 EUR.
3. Enter the guest details, accept the terms, and click Confirm Booking.
   Expected: the confirmation says "Your request was successfully sent." and a booking reference is shown.
